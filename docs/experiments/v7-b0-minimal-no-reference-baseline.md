# V7 B0 minimal no-reference baseline

Primary status: **B0_PAIRED_ONLY_NOT_NO_REFERENCE**

This is a bounded development diagnostic. It is not a full-video detector, a
sealed test, or a formal detector claim.

## 1. Research Question

The preceding paired B0 pilot showed a positive manipulation-specific change
relative to a paired real video. This run asks whether the same frozen B0
state produces stable anomaly evidence for a held-out source when its ruler is
fit only from other real sources, without a paired real reference.

## 2. Why B0 Must Be Tested Before New Dynamics

The test isolates the existing zero-order structural state before adding any
new temporal order, NSI, fusion, or learned classifier. A positive result
would establish a useful no-reference structural baseline; a negative result
would prevent interpreting paired sensitivity as cross-source authenticity
evidence.

## 3. Frozen B0 Definition

The state is exactly

```text
S_t = [mean, std, p25, p75]
```

For each persisted dynamic component and timestamp, pairwise Euclidean
distances are divided by that component's median valid pair distance, exactly
as in the prior `structure_state` implementation. The percentile convention is
NumPy `percentile` (linear interpolation, default method). A component/time
state is valid only when at least three valid particles exist and the median
distance is finite and positive. Missing states are not filled. A window B0
vector is the coordinate-wise median over its persisted K0_S component/time
observations; no component or observation receives extra weight.

The 192 historical window medians were reproduced from
`frontend/window_results.json` against the historical
`metrics/per_window_signal.csv`: 192/192 matched, maximum absolute error
`0.0`. No frontend, video, depth, tracking, pose, or NPZ was reread.

## 4. Frozen Development Population

The exact annotation-selected population was reused: 16 source pairs and 192
role windows (three MANIP and three CTRL anchors for each real/fake side).
Source identities, timestamps, component observations, and validity were not
reselected or filtered. There were 9,722 finite K0_S observations and 174
scored windows. Source `0HV07` had no finite B0 observation and remains in the
population accounting as unavailable; it is not silently removed. Source
`01KML` and `0LNLR` retain their partial window coverage.

This is a 16-source development diagnostic with annotation-selected windows;
it is **not full-video**, **not a sealed test**, and **not a formal detector
claim**.

## 5. No-Reference LOSO Protocol

Each source was held out in turn. A fold's calibration used only finite B0
observations from real windows of the other sources. Held-out real observations
were scored after calibration and never fitted; fake observations were scored
after calibration and never fitted. MANIP/CTRL labels, fake labels, generator,
operation, paired real values, and anomaly scores were not inputs to the
scorer. They are opened only after all scores are fixed for development
evaluation.

All 16 folds were retained. Valid held-out sources had 14 training real
sources; the unavailable `0HV07` fold had 15 training real sources and zero
held-out observations. Training real observation counts ranged from 4,110 to
4,638. Every fold records `fake_fitting_count=0` and
`held_out_real_fitting_count=0` in
`calibration/per_fold_support.csv`.

## 6. Robust Real-Only Scorer

The sole scorer is the pre-declared Robust Diagonal B0 Score. Each training
source has equal total mass, with observations within a source weighted
equally. The source-balanced weighted empirical quantile is the first sorted
value whose cumulative mass reaches the requested quantile. For each
dimension:

```text
m_d = source-balanced median
s_d = 1.4826 * source-balanced MAD
```

If a MAD scale is at or below `1e-12`, the fixed fallback is IQR/1.349; if
that is also unavailable, the fixed scale floor is `1e-6`. For a held-out
observation:

```text
z_d = (S_d - m_d) / (s_d + 1e-8)
A_B0 = sqrt(mean(z_d ** 2))
```

Window and source summaries use medians of valid observations and windows,
respectively. No covariance, Mahalanobis distance, PCA, feature weighting,
post-hoc dimension selection, or paired distance is used for inference.

## 7. Held-out Real Behavior

Across the 15 sources with valid real B0 scores, source-level real window
medians have median `1.005380`, MAD `0.292908`, and p10--p90
`[0.511395, 1.576264]`. Per-source real score summaries are retained in
`evaluation/b0_no_reference_summary.json`; source `0HV07` is explicitly shown
with zero valid windows. The spread is descriptive evidence that the simple
cross-source ruler is not source invariant.

## 8. Held-out Fake Scoring

All fake scores were generated by the same fixed real-only LOSO rulers. No
fake value entered any scaler. The score tables are
`scores/per_observation_b0_scores.csv`, `scores/per_window_b0_scores.csv`, and
`scores/per_source_b0_scores.csv`.

## 10. Source-Level Stability

The source-level statistical unit is the source, not the number of windows or
observations. The 15 valid sources have mixed delta signs (7/15 positive for
both Delta_manip and J_B0), and the intervals include zero. Equal source
weighting prevents a source with more component observations from dominating;
the result is therefore not a one- or two-source artifact by weighting. The
large cross-source real spread and mixed directions indicate that this simple
ruler is unstable for the frozen development population.

## 11. Comparison with Historical Paired B0

The historical paired B0 result was `G_B0` median `1.117231`, positive
fraction `0.928571`, exploratory paired AUROC `0.747619`. Those values measure
matched fake-versus-paired-real sensitivity. The present no-reference result
has negative median `Delta_manip` and `J_B0`, positive fraction `0.466667`, and
exploratory fake-manipulation versus real-manipulation AUROC `0.442929`.
Scores are on different scales and their magnitudes are not compared. The
direction and source-level stability do show that paired sensitivity did not
transfer to a stable no-reference discrimination rule here.

## 12. Limitations

This is a small 16-source, annotation-selected development population rather
than full-video evaluation or a sealed test. One source has no B0 observation
and two sources have partial coverage. The experiment reuses persisted
frontend-derived B0 observations, tests one predeclared diagonal robust ruler,
and does not establish frontend accuracy, causal full-video behavior, or a
general detector. No source, action, generator, operation, or validity case
was removed after seeing results.

## 13. Scientific Conclusion

The historical paired B0 sensitivity was **not converted into stable
cross-source no-reference structural anomaly evidence** by the fixed robust
real-only LOSO scorer. The primary status is
`B0_PAIRED_ONLY_NOT_NO_REFERENCE`. This result is about the current B0
representation plus this minimal calibration rule; it does not prove that all
future B0 calibration or supervised methods are impossible. The observed
difficulty is consistent with cross-source calibration/heterogeneity in this
pilot, while representation limitation cannot be separated conclusively from
the small development population.
